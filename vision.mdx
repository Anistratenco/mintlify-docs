## Health should feel continuous

Human biology does not reset between appointments. Sleep, work, relationships, surroundings, measurements, and medical records all belong to the same life. The systems supporting that life should be able to hold the wider picture.

## Intelligence that can move with you

HealthOS is the foundation beneath the Health Assistant. The longer-term aim is to connect context, tools, and evidence so a useful conversation can carry forward without making you reconstruct your story each time.

We want health intelligence to remain useful as your life changes. That means connecting new information with earlier context, preserving sources, and leaving room for uncertainty and correction. This is a product ambition, not a description of current memory controls.

## Solving health is the ambition

Better health begins with better understanding: information personal enough to matter, careful enough to inspect, and clear enough to use. “Solving health” names the horizon we work toward, not a claim that one answer can finish the work.

Today, the Health Assistant helps you organize information, explore evidence, and use context you share in conversations and files. The wider platform—portable health intelligence, deeper preventive understanding, and future products—remains work in progress.

Read the fuller story on our [About page](https://anistratenco.com/about), or [start with one question](/quickstart) that matters to you.
