## Health should feel continuous

Your health does not begin and end with an appointment. It lives in your sleep, your surroundings, your relationships, and the small choices you make every day.

We are building the Health Assistant to help you understand that bigger picture. A place to ask without feeling lost, keep useful context, and turn complicated information into questions and next steps you can understand.

## Intelligence that stays with you

HealthOS is the foundation beneath the assistant. Its purpose is to connect useful context with tools and evidence, so each conversation can build on what matters rather than starting your story again.

The direction is person-centered and increasingly decentralized: useful intelligence should support your agency, not concentrate control over your health story. That is a design ambition—not a claim that today’s app runs entirely on your device or is a blockchain DApp.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand">
    Start with a question in your own words. Ask for an explanation that makes sense to you, and look at the evidence behind important claims.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reflect">
    Bring in the context you want to share. Notice what has changed, what remains uncertain, and what deserves a conversation with a professional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose">
    Use what you learn to prepare your next step. You remain the decision-maker; the assistant is here to support your understanding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## A bigger ambition, an honest starting point

Our mission is to help solve health. That means working toward a future where trustworthy health information is easier for everyone to understand and use. It is an ambition, not a promise to prevent every illness or guarantee an outcome.

Today, the Health Assistant helps you organize information, explore evidence, and prepare better questions. Diagnosis, prescribing, treatment, and clinical monitoring remain with qualified professionals. Future products and integrations are not available simply because they are part of our vision.

Read the full story on our [About page](https://anistratenco.com/about), or [get started](/quickstart) with one question that matters to you.

<h2 id="page-questions">Questions? Answers.</h2>
<Accordion title="Does preventive mean predicting every health problem?">
No. It means making useful information easier to understand before and between moments of care. It does not replace professional advice or recommended screening.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I control what the assistant remembers?">
Review saved information in [Memory history](/memory). Correct inaccurate details and read the difference between suppressing and forgetting before making a change.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is the Health Assistant my doctor?">
No. The current consumer platform is not a licensed medical-care service or an emergency service. Seek appropriate professional help for urgent or high-stakes health concerns.
</Accordion>
