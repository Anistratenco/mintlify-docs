<h2 id="page-questions">Questions? Answers.</h2>

  <Accordion title="Does preventive mean predicting every health problem?">
    No. It means making information and useful questions easier to understand before and between moments of care. It does not mean the assistant can predict every condition, guarantee an outcome, or replace screening recommended by a qualified professional.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Anistratenco my doctor?">
    No. The current consumer platform is not a licensed medical-care or regulated clinical service. Diagnosis, prescribing, treatment, and clinical monitoring belong with qualified professionals.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if an answer gets something wrong?">
    Correct it, check its sources, and avoid acting on uncertain high-stakes information. If the problem concerns the product itself, [contact us](/contact). If saved context is wrong, [review your memory](/memory) too.
  </Accordion>

Your health is more than a single question. It includes what you have experienced, what matters to you, and what changes over time.

Anistratenco is a preventive health intelligence platform. We want to make health information easier to understand, easier to connect, and more useful in everyday life.

## Three ideas guide us

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Evidence stays visible" icon="magnifying-glass">
    You should be able to inspect what supports an answer—not just accept a confident explanation.

  <Card title="Context carries forward" icon="brain">

## What that means today

Use the Health Assistant to organize information, explore a question, understand evidence, and prepare for a conversation with a qualified professional.

| You want to… | A useful starting point |
| --- | --- |
| Understand an unfamiliar explanation | Ask for plain language and the sources behind it. |
| Prepare for an appointment | Ask for a short list of questions based on the context you choose to share. |
| Keep track of relevant context | [Review your memory](/memory) and correct information that is no longer accurate. |
| Make answers easier to use | Set your [answer preferences](/answer-preferences), then refine the answer in conversation. |

<Tip>
  Start with one goal. “Help me understand this” is enough. You can add detail as the conversation develops.
</Tip>

## Where we want to go

Our longer-term ambition is a more continuous understanding of health: information that remains connected to its context, sources, and changes over time.
