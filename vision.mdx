## Health should feel continuous

Your health does not begin at an appointment and disappear when you leave. It moves through sleep, work, relationships, surroundings, and the choices that fill an ordinary day.

Most health software sees a fragment. We want the Health Assistant to help you hold the wider picture: ask without feeling lost, connect information over time, and arrive at your next decision with better questions.

## Intelligence that can move with you

HealthOS is the foundation beneath the assistant. It is designed to connect context, tools, and evidence so a useful conversation can carry forward instead of making you explain your story from the beginning each time.

Our direction is person-owned and increasingly decentralized. In time, health intelligence should be portable across the services you trust and less dependent on any single gatekeeper. That is the DApp ambition: more control at the edges, clear permission at every boundary, and a system that can work around the person. The current app is not fully decentralized, self-hosted, or a blockchain product.

## Solving health is the ambition

We believe better health begins with better understanding: information that is personal enough to matter, careful enough to trust, and clear enough to use. “Solving health” is the horizon we work toward—not a promise that software can prevent every illness or guarantee an outcome.

Today, the Health Assistant helps you organize information, explore evidence, and prepare better questions. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace clinical care. Future products and integrations remain future work until they are actually available.
