Suggest a feature or share public product feedback on the [UserJot board](https://anistratenco.userjot.com).

<Warning>
  Posts on the board are public. Do not include health records, private conversation details, passwords, credentials, recovery codes, or payment information. For account help or a private bug report, use the [contact form](https://anistratenco.com/contact).
</Warning>

## Before you post

Describe the problem you want to solve and keep the details safe to share publicly. For service interruptions, check the [service status page](https://status.anistratenco.com).

<section className="help-faq" aria-labelledby="page-questions">
<h2 id="page-questions">Questions and answers</h2>
<Accordion title="Who can read feedback on the board?">The UserJot board is public. Assume your post can be read by anyone who visits it.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where should I ask for help with my account?">Use the private [contact form](https://anistratenco.com/contact) for account help, billing questions, or a private bug report.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I include a health record to explain a feature idea?">No. Describe the goal or problem without health records or private conversation details.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where do I send a feature suggestion?">Use the public UserJot board and share only information that is safe to post publicly.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Where can I check for service interruptions?">Check the Anistratenco service status page for availability updates.</Accordion>
</section>
