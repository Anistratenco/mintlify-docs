Voice should make a conversation easier to begin, not harder to trust. Speak when that feels more natural, read the transcript before you rely on it, and choose how the Health Assistant sounds when it reads an answer back.

Open your profile menu, then go to **Settings → Voice**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on Voice mode">
    Voice mode places the microphone controls beside the message box. Turning it off hides those controls and stops an active recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a microphone">
    Leave **System default** selected, or choose a specific input when your device has more than one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test before you record">
    Select **Test** and speak at a comfortable volume. The moving level bars confirm that the browser is receiving sound. Select **Stop** when you are finished; a test never sends a message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the transcript">
    Names, dates, measurements, and medication doses deserve a second look. Correct the text before sending whenever a changed word could change the meaning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Speaking and listening are separate

<div className="mint-grid mint-grid-cols-1 md:mint-grid-cols-2">
  <div className="help-panel">
    ### Speak to the Assistant

    **Voice mode** records speech for transcription. You remain in control of the message and can review the words before they enter the conversation.
  </div>
  <div className="help-panel">
    ### Listen to an answer

    **Read responses aloud** plays new completed answers. It does not turn on the microphone. You can also use **Read aloud** on an individual response.
  </div>
</div>

## Know what the microphone is telling you

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Not checked">
    The browser has not tested the selected microphone in this session. Select **Test** when you are ready.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Checking">
    The browser is requesting access and opening the input. Finish any browser permission prompt before trying again.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Connected">
    The input is live for the test. Speak and watch the level bars; then select **Stop**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Permission blocked">
    Microphone access was declined or is blocked for this site. Allow microphone access in the browser's site settings, return to Anistratenco, and select **Test** again.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="No microphone found">
    The selected input is no longer available. Reconnect it or choose another microphone, then test again.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<div className="help-panel">
  A successful test proves that the browser can hear the selected input. It does not prove that every word will be transcribed correctly. Quiet rooms, a steady distance from the microphone, and a quick review still matter.
</div>

## Teach transcription the words that matter to you

The **Transcription dictionary** gives the speech system hints for names and vocabulary it may not expect. Add a person, medicine, organization, or specialist term exactly as you want it recognized.

<div className="mint-grid mint-grid-cols-1 md:mint-grid-cols-2">
  <div className="help-panel">
    ### What fits

    Add up to 100 entries. Each entry can contain 50 characters, with 2,048 characters across the dictionary.
  </div>
  <div className="help-panel">
    ### What happens twice

    Empty entries and duplicates are ignored. Capitalization alone does not create a second entry.
  </div>
</div>

Dictionary entries are suggestions, not replacements. They improve context for transcription but do not guarantee a spelling. Clearing the dictionary offers an undo action, so an accidental reset can be restored while that action is still available.

## Keep only the transcripts you need

Recent transcripts are a working surface, not a second chat archive. The latest 20 completed transcripts are kept in encrypted, account-scoped storage for this browser. From the list, you can copy one transcript or delete it individually.

<div className="help-panel">
  ### Clean up after yourself

  Turn on **Clean up transcripts** when you want the app to reduce obvious transcription clutter. Keep it off when preserving the raw wording matters more. In either case, review health details before sending.
</div>

If encrypted local storage is unavailable, the Voice workspace reports that the settings cannot be loaded on this device. Changing browsers or clearing browser data can also remove this local history. Messages you already sent remain governed by the conversation and data controls described in [Data, backups, and deletion](/data).

## Choose how answers sound

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Browser TTS">
    Uses the voices installed or exposed by your browser and device. **Language** filters that list; it does not translate an answer. Choosing another language clears the previous voice selection so you can choose a compatible voice.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Speak Aloud API">
    Uses the higher-quality cloud speech option. Availability depends on the service and your connection. The browser-specific language and voice selectors are not shown for this engine.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

| Control | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| Playback speed | Choose 0.75×, 1×, 1.25×, 1.5×, or 2×. |
| Sound effects | Plays the small audio cues around recording. |
| Enhanced audio | Uses browser echo cancellation, noise suppression, and automatic gain control when supported. It is on by default. |
| Dynamic normalization | Smooths sudden volume changes before audio reaches transcription. It is off by default. |

These controls change capture and playback. They do not change the meaning, evidence, or safety limits of an answer.

## Privacy deserves a quiet boundary

Microphone access is granted to the browser, and the native browser prompt is the authority for that permission. A microphone test stays in Settings and does not create a chat message. Recording for transcription sends only when you use the product flow that performs transcription; a cloud speech engine may process the answer text needed to produce audio.

Do not paste a transcript into public feedback. For private account help, use [Contact us](/contact) and leave out passwords, authentication codes, recovery material, and anything the support team does not need.

<div className="help-faq">
  ## Questions about Voice

  <AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Why can I see the microphone but not record?">
      Test the selected microphone in **Settings → Voice**. If it says **Permission blocked**, allow access in the browser's site settings. If it says **No microphone found**, reconnect the input or choose another one.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does Read responses aloud listen to me?">
      No. Read-aloud controls playback of completed answers. Voice mode controls microphone recording and transcription.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Will the dictionary always spell a term correctly?">
      No. Dictionary entries are recognition hints. Review the transcript, especially when a name, number, measurement, or dose affects the meaning.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Where are recent transcripts stored?">
      The latest 20 are kept in encrypted, account-scoped storage for the current browser. They are not a substitute for your conversation archive and may disappear when local browser data is cleared.
    </Accordion>
  </AccordionGroup>
</div>
