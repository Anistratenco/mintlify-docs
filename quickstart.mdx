Anistratenco is building health intelligence around the person rather than the institution. The Health Assistant is the first way into that work: a place to bring together questions, files, measurements, lived context, and the evidence behind an answer. You do not need to know the medical words before you begin.

HealthOS is the system beneath the conversation. It connects tools, permissions, saved context, and evidence so an answer can become part of a longer understanding instead of disappearing at the end of a chat. Today, the Health Assistant helps you organize information and prepare better questions and decisions. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace qualified care.

## Begin with what matters

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Health Assistant">
    [Go to Chat](https://anistratenco.com/chat) and sign in. If the navigation is out of view, select **Expand sidebar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make a fresh start">
    Select **New chat**. Write in **Chat message**, then select **Send message**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add only what helps">
    Give the Assistant the context that changes the answer. Attach a relevant file, name the outcome you want, or ask it to keep the explanation short.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay in the conversation">
    Ask a follow-up. Correct anything that is wrong. For an important claim, open the cited source and check the original context.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A useful first message can be simple: “Help me prepare five questions for my next appointment.”
</Tip>

**Add attachment** opens the available file options. **Record voice** starts voice input and may trigger your browser's microphone permission. Neither action sends a message until you continue through its product flow.

<Note>
  Names, dates, measurements, and medication details deserve a second look. A polished answer or transcript can still be wrong.
</Note>

## Let understanding carry forward

Health rarely fits into one conversation. A previous result can give a new measurement context; a correction can change what the Assistant should remember; a project can keep one goal from becoming mixed with another. Continuity is useful only when it remains inspectable and under your control.

## Treat an answer as working material

Use **Copy** when you need the text elsewhere. Use **Continue in new chat** to branch from an answer without losing the conversation you came from. Your own messages can also show **Edit** and **Delete**; completed Assistant messages can show **Read aloud**. The available actions depend on the message and its state.

<Warning>
  If a response fails, keep the visible reference ID and use **Share feedback about this error** when it appears. Before retrying a request that could send, book, buy, or change something outside Anistratenco, check whether the first action completed.
</Warning>
