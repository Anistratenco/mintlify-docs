Make the app easier to read and use. These settings change how Anistratenco looks, not your conversation content.

Open your profile menu in the sidebar, choose **Settings**, then **Appearance**.

## Light, Dark, and Automatic

Choose **Light** for a light background or **Dark** for a dark background. **Automatic** follows your device's light or dark appearance and updates when that preference changes. Your theme choice is saved in this browser.

## Accent

Choose blue, purple, pink, orange, or green, or open **Custom accent color**. The accent highlights controls and selected parts of the interface; it does not change the meaning of a response.

**Automatic accent** leaves accent styling to the theme. It is separate from Automatic light/dark appearance.

## Font size

Increase **Font size** when text feels too small. Use the slider or enter a number from **14 to 18**. The markers are **Standard**, **Large**, and **Largest**.

This scales text presentation, not message content. The reset beside the slider returns it to **14** without resetting your other choices.

## Contrast

Raise **Contrast** to make supported secondary text more prominent. The range is **0 to 100**, with **Standard**, **Medium**, and **High** markers.

This changes app styling, not screen brightness. It does not change every image or embedded item. The individual reset returns it to **0**.

## Control size

Increase **Control size** if supported buttons and controls are difficult to target. The range is **0.75× to 1.5×**, with **1×** as Standard.

This is separate from Font size. Its reset returns control scale to **1×**.

## Reduce motion

Limit interface animations and transitions. Progress and state changes remain visible so you can tell when something is loading or has finished.

## Reset appearance

Restore all appearance defaults together: **Automatic** theme, **Blue** accent, font size **14**, contrast **0**, control size **1×**, and Reduce motion **off**.

This does not delete conversations, files, or your account. To undo only one slider change, use that slider's reset instead.

## Get help

Change one option at a time so you can judge the result. For text that is cut off or a control that becomes unusable, [contact us](https://anistratenco.com/contact) and choose **Report a bug**. Include the setting and your browser or device. Remove private information from screenshots.
