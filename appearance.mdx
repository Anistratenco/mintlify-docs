<Visibility for="agents">
These are appearance controls, not changes to answer content or medical interpretation. Open the profile menu, Settings, Appearance. Theme is stored in the current browser. Font size ranges from 14 to 18 in steps of 1; contrast from 0 to 100 in steps of 5; control scale from 0.75 to 1.5 in steps of 0.05. Defaults are Automatic theme, Blue accent, 14 font size, 0 contrast, 1 control scale, Reduce motion off. Recheck visible controls before giving navigation advice if the interface changes. Examples here are local documentation previews, not connected account controls.
</Visibility>

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Appearance" icon="palette">Open your profile menu in the sidebar, choose **Settings**, then **Appearance**.</Step>
<Step title="Change one thing" icon="sliders">Start with the theme or text size. Read a familiar conversation to judge whether the change helps.</Step>
<Step title="Keep what works" icon="check">Use the reset beside a slider to undo that adjustment without changing the others.</Step>
</Steps>

## Light, Dark, and Automatic

Choose **Light** for a light background or **Dark** for a dark background. **Automatic** follows your device's appearance and updates when it changes. Your theme choice is saved in this browser.

## Font size

Use the slider or enter a number from **14 to 18**. The markers are **Standard**, **Large**, and **Largest**. This scales interface text, not the content of your messages. The individual reset returns it to **14**.

## Contrast and control size

| Setting | What changes | Range | Individual reset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Contrast** | Strengthens supported text and surface separation in both themes. It does not change screen brightness or every image. | 0–100, in steps of 5 | 0 |
| **Control size** | Scales supported buttons and controls. Use it when targets feel too small. | 0.75×–1.5×, in steps of 0.05 | 1× |

Control size is separate from Font size. You can make text easier to read without making the same change to controls.

## Reduce motion

Turn on **Reduce motion** to limit decorative movement. Progress and state changes remain visible, so you can still tell when something is loading or has finished.

## Reset appearance

**Reset appearance** restores the defaults together: **Automatic** theme, **Blue** accent, font size **14**, contrast **0**, control size **1×**, and Reduce motion **off**. The reset is shown when an appearance choice differs from its default.

<Note>This does not delete your conversations, files, or account. Use an individual slider's reset when you want to undo only one adjustment.</Note>

## Get help

If text is cut off or a control becomes difficult to use, [Contact us](https://anistratenco.com/contact) and choose **Report a bug**. Include the setting, browser, and device. Remove private information from screenshots.
