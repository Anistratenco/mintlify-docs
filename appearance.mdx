These are appearance controls, not changes to answer content or medical interpretation. Open the profile menu, Settings, Appearance. Theme is stored in the current browser. Font size ranges from 14 to 18 in steps of 1; contrast from 0 to 100 in steps of 5; control scale from 0.75 to 1.5 in steps of 0.05. Open your profile menu, then **Settings → Appearance**. These settings change the interface, not the content of an answer.

## See the choices together

Try the theme cards, accent swatches, and sliders. They change only this sample—not your account or the rest of this guide.

<AppearancePreview />

## Light, Dark, and Automatic

Choose Light or Dark to keep one appearance. Automatic follows your device, including changes to its light or dark setting. Your choice is saved in this browser.

| Choice | Canvas and text | What stays the same |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light | Pale surfaces with dark text | Your content and chosen accent |
| Dark | Dark surfaces with light text | Your content and chosen accent |
| Automatic | Follows your device’s Light or Dark preference | Your content and chosen accent |

Theme changes the canvas, reading surfaces, text, and control edges together. Automatic follows a device schedule if you have one; it does not create a schedule itself.

## Accent

Your accent marks selected controls and other interface details. Choose a preset or enter your own color in Custom accent color.

The presets are Blue, Purple, Pink, Orange, and Green. Automatic accent uses the theme’s neutral treatment—dark in Light mode, light in Dark mode. It is separate from Automatic theme. Try your accent against both themes so selected controls remain easy to distinguish.

## Font size

Move the slider or enter a number from 14 to 18. The markers are Standard, Large, and Largest. This scales interface text without rewriting your messages. Resetting this slider returns it to 14.

The interface uses Inter. Larger text wraps sooner and takes more vertical space. Move the preview’s slider to compare the same paragraph. Browser zoom also affects what you see, so check your choice in a real conversation.

## Contrast and control size

| Setting | What changes | Range | Individual reset |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contrast | Strengthens supported text and surface separation in both themes. It does not change screen brightness or every image. | 0–100, in steps of 5 | 0 |
| Control size | Scales supported buttons and controls. Use it when targets feel too small. | 0.75×–1.5×, in steps of 0.05 | 1× |

Control size is separate from Font size. You can make text easier to read without making the same change to controls.

## Reduce motion

Turn on Reduce motion if movement is distracting or uncomfortable. It limits decorative animation while keeping progress and state changes visible.

## Reset appearance

**Reset appearance** restores the defaults together: Automatic theme, Blue accent, font size 14, contrast 0, control size 1×, and Reduce motion off. The reset appears when a choice differs from its default.

<Note>This does not delete your conversations, files, or account. Use an individual slider's reset when you want to undo only one adjustment.</Note>

## Get help

If text is cut off or a control becomes difficult to use, [Contact us](https://anistratenco.com/contact) and choose **Report a bug**. Include the setting, browser, and device. Remove private information from screenshots.

<Accordion title="Do these previews change my account?">No. They only demonstrate appearance in this guide. Make your real changes in Settings → Appearance.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does Automatic change with my device?">Yes. Automatic theme follows the light or dark preference reported by your device. Automatic accent is a separate choice.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Will resetting appearance remove my information?">No. It restores appearance choices, not chats, files, or account data.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does text size change the content of an answer?">No. It changes how the interface is displayed. It does not shorten, rewrite, or regenerate an answer.</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens when reduced motion is on?">Supported interface movement becomes quieter or stops. The setting does not disable product features or change saved information.</Accordion>
