<section className="help-faq">

<Accordion title="Live has no usable reading">
Check macOS camera permission, the selected camera and the **When to track** rules. Resume Eye if it is paused. Sit in even light and keep both eyes in view. Check again without a competing app using the selected camera. [Camera and tracking](/camera)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A blink does not clear the dimming immediately">
First check that this is an actual blink reminder and that Live still registers your blink. Leave the reminder Preview, check the camera framing and try with steady lighting. If the issue repeats, note whether Live showed a gap at the time. [Reminders](/reminders)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Eye tracks me, but my history looks empty">
Check that there was enough usable tracking for the period and look for gaps in coverage. A Watch Together session with two detected viewers pauses saved personal history for that camera session. [Today, Live and trends](/dashboard)
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My sign-in or download fails">
Check which build you installed and which Anistratenco account holds access. Consult the [shared status page](https://status.anistratenco.com/) for service incidents and the [Download page](https://geteye.app/download/) for verified installers. [Account and updates](/account-and-updates)
</Accordion>

</section>

For [private support](https://geteye.app/contact/), include the Eye version, macOS version, camera type, what you were doing and what happened. For a reminder issue, say which cue was enabled and whether Live was usable. Keep passwords, payment details, camera footage and personal history out of public feedback.
