## Choose the camera

In **Settings → General → Tracking**, use **Camera** to select a connected camera or choose **Automatic**. If macOS denied access, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera**, enable Eye if listed, then reopen Eye if macOS asks you to. The camera setting and the macOS permission are separate.

Open **Live** while seated the way you normally work. Keep both eyes in view and use even front lighting. If readings disappear, check for backlight, glare, a blocked camera or another app using the selected camera. Resolve camera and framing problems before adjusting **Blink sensitivity**. Raising it can pick up smaller eyelid movements, so change it a little at a time and check several natural blinks in Live.

## Read the tracking state

A visible face does not guarantee usable eye measurements. Live can go quiet while the camera starts, the eyes leave view or the readings are unusable. A missing observation means Eye has no usable value for that interval. Compare the **Tracking coverage** card with daily counts before drawing conclusions about a quiet day.

**Tracking issue alerts** can sound when a camera, face or tracking delay issue persists. They use the reminder sound setting. Check the visible state in Live first so you can tell which condition needs attention.

## Choose when it runs

In **Settings → General → When to track**, set the power rules for battery and external power and the weekly time windows. **Pause now** uses your selected **Pause duration**. Choose **Until I resume** if you want to decide when to resume. When a temporary pause ends, Eye still follows the power and weekly rules. You can revoke camera access in macOS independently of all Eye settings.
