## Set up a blink reminder

In **Settings → Reminders → Screen reminders**, turn on **Dim screen** and set **Time without blinking**. Choose a **Dim level**, **Fade color** and **Dim fade time**. Use **Preview** to see the fade. Preview helps you choose a comfortable cue. For the actual response to a blink, leave the preview and let Eye track normally.

The reminder needs usable tracking and a schedule that allows delivery. When Eye accepts a blink that satisfies a visible full screen or split reminder, the affected overlay clears immediately. Tracking loss, a manual clear and a timeout follow the regular fade. If the overlay fades instead of clearing on a blink, open **Live** and check whether Eye still sees both eyes and registers the blink.

## Pick another cue

**Reminder sounds** lets you choose the sound and volume. With **Record**, Eye asks for microphone access and can record up to eight seconds for a local reminder sound. Cancel discards the temporary recording. In **Haptics**, choose whether you want a tap on a detected blink, when a blink is due or with particular notifications. **Blink notifications** use macOS delivery and sound settings, including Focus. The app issues at most one blink notification per minute.

## Schedule movement and head angle reminders

In **Reminder schedule**, decide when cues should run. These rules affect delivery independently of the tracking schedule. **Movement reminder** counts active Mac use while tracking and reminders are allowed. It stops counting after one minute without input and resets after two minutes, on lock or when you pause tracking. Choose **Break**, **Stand** or **Stretch** as the activity.

**Head angle reminders** let you choose a nod angle, a tilt angle and **Hold before reminding**. The cue reacts to a sustained measured angle. Adjust it for how you sit and position your camera.
