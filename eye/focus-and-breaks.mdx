## Focus timer

Add the **Focus timer** widget from the Today gallery. In **Settings → General → Focus timer**, set the durations for focus, short break and long break. Start the timer from the widget and use its controls to move between work and break phases. The timer is separate from both blink tracking and the reminders schedule.

## Breathe and Eye exercise

Add **Breathe** to Today and start a session from the widget. In the full screen view, adjust the duration or choose the breathing motion in settings. Follow the expansion and contraction at a comfortable pace. The stop control ends and resets a breathing session. Start again for a new session.

Use the **Eye exercise** card for a short guided break. You can also set a recurring movement reminder to prompt a screen break, stretch or standing interval. [Configure movement reminders](/reminders#schedule-movement-and-head-angle-reminders).
