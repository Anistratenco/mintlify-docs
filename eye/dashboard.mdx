## Today

**Blinks today** counts recognized blinks over the usable tracking Eye saved today. **Blink rate** expresses observed blinks per tracked minute. A daily rate needs at least 30 seconds of tracking. **Tracking coverage** is the share of the active Mac time Eye recorded today that had usable eye tracking. For example, 15 tracked minutes during 60 recorded active minutes gives 25% coverage. Eye measures active time while it runs.

A zero is a measured value. A dash or gap means the required observations are missing or too short. Check coverage before comparing two days. A lower count can simply mean Eye observed a smaller part of the day.

## Live

Use **Live** to see what Eye is observing now. Eye opening shows readings for the left and right eyes. A blink can appear as a brief drop. A gap can mean tracking was unavailable. Camera and measurement widgets help distinguish a live camera, usable observations and the timing of delivery. Head movement requires usable pose readings over time.

## Longer views

Use the history charts to compare rate, tracked time and daily totals over their selected range. **Eye health** compares today’s blink rate with your personal reference. **Eye stress** uses recent blink rate and time between blinks and needs at least 15 seconds of tracking in the last minute. **Eye strain** weighs the last 30 minutes toward recent activity. **Longevity** summarizes blink consistency across tracked time. These are Eye’s estimates of blink habits. Use them to compare your own observations, never for clinical decisions.

With **Expression estimates** enabled, expression history shows an estimate of visible facial expressions. It cannot establish how someone feels. The shared viewing session is excluded.
