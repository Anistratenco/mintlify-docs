## Find a setting

Choose **Settings…** from the Eye menu, or press **Command + comma** while Eye is active. Use **General** for the camera, tracking hours, pause duration, automatic launch, account access and focus timing. **Reminders** controls when you get a cue. **Haptics** controls when Eye uses a compatible trackpad. [Camera and tracking](/camera) [Blink and movement reminders](/reminders)

**Appearance** changes Eye itself, including the theme, text size and Island glass. **Display** changes screen tint and software dimming. Use **History** to export or delete saved readings and **Updates** to check the release feed. **Developer** becomes available after you enable **Developer Mode** in General.
