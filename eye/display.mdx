---
title: Display profiles
description: Adjust soft screen tint and dimming by app, place, time or available light.
icon: monitor
---

Open **Settings → Display**. Under **Display Profiles**, choose **Default** in **Editing profile**, then set **Screen tint**, **Tint strength** and **Software dimming**. Software dimming changes the image on your screen while hardware brightness stays where you set it. Use **Reset display** to clear your changes or **macOS Color Filters…** to open the system controls.

Eye uses the first matching profile, starting with **App Override**, followed by **Bedtime**, **Saved Place** and **Default**. Use **Add App Override…** to keep an app neutral when you need accurate color while tinting the rest of your workspace.

Turn on **Schedule Bedtime**, set **Start**, **Wake** and **Fade duration**, then select **Edit Bedtime Appearance**. Bedtime follows your Mac’s local time. Under **Saved Place**, use **Save Current Location…** and grant location access to save a place on your Mac. Eye can apply that profile within 500 metres of the saved place.

In **Adaptive dimming**, **Dim in low light** uses eligible camera readings to darken the displayed image slightly. If the display looks different than expected, check **Active Appearance** and which app is in front.
