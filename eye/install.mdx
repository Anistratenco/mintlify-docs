## Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your installer">
    Open [Eye for Mac](https://geteye.app/download/). In the **Apple menu → About This Mac**, an Apple **Chip** means you need the Apple silicon build. An Intel **Processor** means you need the Intel build. Check the macOS requirement next to the installer. If no installer appears, public downloads are closed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move Eye to Applications">
    In **Downloads**, double-click the `.dmg` and drag **Eye** into **Applications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Eye">
    Launch Eye from **Applications**. If macOS shows a warning you don’t recognize, check the download instructions or [contact support](https://geteye.app/contact/).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirm tracking

Open Eye from the menu bar. Give Eye camera access when you’re ready, then open **Live** with your face comfortably in view. Wait for a usable reading and blink naturally. Check for a corresponding change in Live. Your daily blink rate needs some usable tracking time before it appears in Today.

If your build offers **Settings → General → Account**, see [Account and updates](/account-and-updates) for sign-in and access. To keep the camera off for now, use **Pause now** in General or leave the permission disabled. [Set up the camera](/camera) covers both paths.
