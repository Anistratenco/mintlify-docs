## Edit the dashboard

Open **Today** or **Live** and use the edit control to enter widget editing. Open **Widgets** to browse or search the gallery. Add the card to the intended section, place it on the grid and use the card’s resize control if it supports multiple sizes. **Done** saves the layout. **Cancel** or Escape leaves an uncommitted editing session. A fixed size card keeps its supported size and the grid resolves collisions with neighboring cards.

Keep primary readings where you glance first. For example, pair **Blinks today** with **Tracking coverage**, or put **Eye opening** beside the camera timing measurements in Live. A graph and a count answer different questions, so choose the smallest size that still shows the detail you need.

## Desktop widgets and Eye Island

For builds with the macOS widgets registered, open **Edit Widgets** from the desktop or Notification Center, find Eye and choose a size. Desktop widgets use a local snapshot of today’s blink count and tracked time, or the recent blink-rate trend. They update separately from the Live dashboard and may show the last delivered snapshot.

In **Settings → General → Show Eye Island**, choose **During reminders**, **Always** or **Off**. In **Appearance**, use **Glass clarity** for the Island and **Reduce transparency** for opaque Eye surfaces. Use **Follow macOS**, **Light** or **Dark** to select the app theme. **Text size** requires macOS 26 or later.
