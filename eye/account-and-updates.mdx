---
title: Account and updates
description: Sign in to check access, understand availability and use verified app updates.
icon: circle-user
---

If your build shows **Settings → General → Account**, use **Sign in** with the Anistratenco account associated with your Eye access. Finish the browser sign-in and return to the app. The account section reports the grant that Eye received. It can show an active Lifetime grant or an expiry date for a trial or Monthly access.

If your access looks wrong, check that you used the same account as the one associated with the purchase or invitation. Refresh access if the build offers it, or sign out and back in. [Plans](https://geteye.app/plans/) shows the current purchase availability. The availability of the account screen alone does not establish that public checkout is open.

Use **Settings → Updates → Check for updates…** to check the release feed, or enable **Update automatically** where a verified feed is available. Use the [Download page](https://geteye.app/download/) to check the published builds. If an update fails, keep the installed copy and [contact Eye](https://geteye.app/contact/) with its version and your Mac architecture.
