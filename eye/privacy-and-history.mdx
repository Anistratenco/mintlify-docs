---
title: Privacy and history
description: See what Eye keeps on your Mac and how to export or delete it.
icon: shield
---

During ordinary tracking, Eye processes camera frames on your Mac without saving camera video or uploading frames or statistics. Live face geometry and individual expression estimates stay in memory. The app saves daily summaries locally, including blink counts, tracked time, closures, lighting and measured head movement. Eye records active Mac time without retaining the input events used to measure it.

**Expression estimates** is optional. When enabled, Eye keeps minute averages for the primary viewer on this Mac for up to 32 days. Watch Together sessions are excluded. In signed builds, the desktop widgets receive a local snapshot through the macOS App Group. They do not receive video.

In **Settings → History**, use **Export history…** for a copy of the daily statistics. The export excludes Emotion History. Use **Delete history…** to clear saved history and confirm the action. Save a copy before deletion if you want one. To stop collecting new measurements, pause tracking or revoke camera access in macOS.

Microphone access is used for the reminder sound recorder when you choose **Record**. Saving or exporting a recording is a separate action from ordinary tracking. Update checks request release information. When you use account access, the sign-in and subscription service makes its own network requests. See the full [Eye privacy notice](https://geteye.app/privacy/).
