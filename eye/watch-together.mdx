---
title: Watch Together
description: Use separate screen cues for two viewers on the same Mac.
icon: users
---

In **Settings → Reminders → Screen reminders**, enable **Dim screen**, then **Watch Together**. Sit where the same camera can see both viewers clearly. When Eye recognizes a pair, cyan and pink cues correspond to the two viewers. Each person can clear their own cue by blinking when Eye has a usable view.

Detecting a pair pauses saved personal history for that camera session. This keeps shared viewing out of your personal history and expression averages. After the shared session, return to one viewer and check Live. If personal history remains paused until the camera session restarts, pause and resume tracking, then check Live again.

If the split cues appear while you’re alone, check for other faces in view and switch off Watch Together when you return to personal use. If the behavior repeats, share the Eye build and camera setup through [private support](https://geteye.app/contact/).
