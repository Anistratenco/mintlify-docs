## Understand your health, one step at a time

Your Health Assistant helps you make sense of health information, prepare questions, and think through your next steps. Start with what matters to you—you do not need to know the right medical words.

## Ask, check, and follow up

Tell the assistant what you want to understand. Add useful context and ask follow-up questions when something is unclear. Ask for a shorter explanation, a checklist, or questions to take to an appointment.

Check important details against their original sources. If something is wrong, say so. An AI answer can be useful without being correct about everything.

## Powered by HealthOS

HealthOS is the foundation beneath the Health Assistant. It brings conversations, tools, and saved context together so the experience can support your health questions over time. You stay in control of what you share and what you keep.

[Explore the product vision](/vision) or [review saved memory](/memory).

## Find the right help

**Something is not working?** [Contact us](/contact) for private support. To report a problem with the app, choose **Report a bug** on the contact form. Include what you tried, what happened, and any error reference.

**Have an idea?** Visit [Feedback](https://anistratenco.userjot.com) to suggest a feature, vote, and follow the roadmap. Explain what you want to do and what would make it easier.

<Note>
  Feature feedback is public. Keep medical records, diagnoses, medication details, passwords, and private screenshots out of public posts.
</Note>

## For urgent health concerns

The Health Assistant is not an emergency service or a replacement for a qualified healthcare professional. For an urgent concern, seek appropriate medical help rather than waiting for a support reply.
