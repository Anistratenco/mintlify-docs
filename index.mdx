## Understand your health, one step at a time

Health information rarely arrives as a neat answer. Your Health Assistant helps you make sense of it, prepare questions, and think through what comes next. Start with what matters to you—you do not need to know the medical words.

## Ask. Check. Keep going.

Tell the assistant what you want to understand and add the context that changes the question. If an answer feels too dense, ask for a shorter explanation, a checklist, or questions to take to an appointment.

Important details still deserve a check against their original sources. If something is wrong, correct it. A confident answer is not the same thing as a complete one.

## Powered by HealthOS

HealthOS brings conversations, tools, and the context you choose into one foundation. It is how the Health Assistant can support a question today and still understand what matters when you return. You remain in control of what you share and keep.

[Explore our vision](/vision) or [review saved memory](/memory).

## Find the right help

If something is not working, [Contact us](/contact) for private support and choose Report a bug. Tell us what you tried, what happened, and any error reference you saw.

If you have an idea, visit [Feedback](https://anistratenco.userjot.com) to suggest it, vote, and follow its progress. Describe what you were trying to do and what would have made it easier.

<Note>Feature feedback is public. Keep medical records, diagnoses, medication details, passwords, and private screenshots out of public posts.</Note>

## For urgent health concerns

The Health Assistant is not an emergency service or a replacement for a qualified healthcare professional. For an urgent concern, seek appropriate medical help rather than waiting for a support reply.
