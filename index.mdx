## From one answer to a clearer picture

One conversation can begin with a symptom, a result, a document, or a decision you are trying to understand. Ask for the form that helps: a shorter explanation, a comparison, a checklist, or questions worth carrying into the next conversation.

Evidence stays connected to the answer so you can inspect it. Corrections can change what the Assistant should carry forward. The aim is not a final pronouncement; it is understanding that becomes more useful over time.

## HealthOS beneath the conversation

HealthOS brings conversations, tools, evidence, and the context you choose into one foundation. It gives the Health Assistant continuity without making memory invisible: you can review what is retained, correct it, export it, or remove it.

[Explore our vision](/vision) or [review saved memory](/memory).

## Help and feedback

If something is not working, [Contact us](/contact) for private support and choose Report a bug. Tell us what you tried, what happened, and any error reference you saw.

If you have an idea, visit [Feedback](https://anistratenco.userjot.com) to suggest it, vote, and follow its progress. Describe what you were trying to do and what would have made it easier.

<Note>Feature feedback is public. Keep medical records, diagnoses, medication details, passwords, and private screenshots out of public posts.</Note>
