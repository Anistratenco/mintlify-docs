## A wider view of your health

A result is more useful when you understand what was measured, when it changed, and what else was happening. Your sleep, habits, goals, and questions can matter alongside a document or a number. Bring the context you want the Assistant to consider.

Ask it to explain a finding, compare sources, organize information, or help you prepare a next step. Open the evidence behind an answer and correct anything that needs attention. Understanding develops through the conversation.

## HealthOS beneath the conversation

HealthOS connects the conversation to tools, evidence, and saved context. Our goal is continuity: useful understanding that can grow as your life changes. Memory stays reviewable, and you choose what to correct, retain, export, or remove.

[Explore our vision](/vision) or [review saved memory](/memory).

## Help and feedback

If something is not working, [Contact us](/contact) for private support and choose Report a bug. Tell us what you tried, what happened, and any error reference you saw.

If you have an idea, visit [Feedback](https://anistratenco.userjot.com) to suggest it, vote, and follow its progress. Describe what you were trying to do and what would have made it easier.

<Note>Feature feedback is public. Keep medical records, diagnoses, medication details, passwords, and private screenshots out of public posts.</Note>
