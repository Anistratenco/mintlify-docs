## Open your archive

Open your profile menu, then **Settings → Archived chats**. Chats archived from History appear here.

## Find a conversation

Search by the chat title or project name. This search does not look through every message in a conversation. Use the project filter to narrow the list to one project or chats with no project.

The sort menu lets you arrange chats by when they were archived, updated, or created, by message count, or alphabetically. **Recently archived** shows the newest additions first.

## Preview a chat

Select a conversation to see its details and a preview of its latest messages. The preview is not the full conversation: it shows up to six messages.

## Restore a chat

Choose the restore action beside the conversation. This takes it out of the archive so you can return to it from your chat history. Restoring is different from creating a new chat.

## Delete an archived chat

Choose the delete action beside the conversation you want to remove. If you made a mistake, use **Undo** in the notification straight away. Undo is available briefly, not as a permanent recovery folder.

## Delete several archived chats

Check the search and project filter before using the bulk delete action. It applies to the chats matching the current view. Clear the filters only if you intend to include all archived chats.

If a deletion fails, read the message in the app. Do not assume it completed just because the row briefly disappeared.

<Warning>
  Deleting is not archiving. Keep anything you need before removing it, and do not rely on being able to recover a chat after the Undo window closes.
</Warning>

For backups and other deletion controls, see [Data, backups, and deletion](/data).

<section className="help-faq" aria-labelledby="page-questions">
<h2 id="page-questions">Questions and Answers</h2>
<Accordion title="Is archiving the same as deleting?">
<div>No. An archived chat can be restored to your history. Deletion is separate and becomes permanent after its brief Undo window.</div>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What does the archive search look through?">
<div>It searches chat titles and project names, not the full text of every message. Check your project filter and sorting if a conversation is hard to find.</div>
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I return an archived chat to the sidebar?"><div>Yes. Open its actions and choose the option to restore or unarchive it. The conversation returns without creating a duplicate.</div></Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does archiving stop work that is still running?"><div>Do not use archiving as a cancellation control. Check the conversation’s current state and cancel or stop the work through the action provided for that task.</div></Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why can’t I find a conversation I just archived?"><div>Clear the search, check the selected project filter, and confirm the sort order. Archive search covers titles and project names rather than every message.</div></Accordion>
</section>
